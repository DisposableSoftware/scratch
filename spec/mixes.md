# Mixes

The **Me** tab: mixes of the user's own music, made on the device from their hearts, their plays, their genres, what they play least and release dates. A mix is a queue the app builds and plays. It is never written to the server, since editing playlists is a non-goal (`spec/product.md`). The model is the on-device language model in iOS (the Foundation Models framework), so no dependency is added and no track data goes to any service but the user's own server.

## Implementation notes

- The model is the Foundation Models framework's `SystemLanguageModel`; the tab is shown when its `availability` is `.available`, checked at launch and whenever the app becomes active. Only a Gateway imports the framework, behind a protocol the tests mock. The simulator may not have the model, in which case the tab is hidden and the model's own behaviour can only be seen on a device.
- Its context is small, so a shortlist is compact (one short line per track) and capped at 60 tracks. The model answers in a typed structure (a name, a description and a list of numbers) with guided generation, so nothing is parsed out of prose. It is given positions in the shortlist, not the server's track IDs, which are long and which it copies badly.
- Tracks gain what the shortlist needs from the server: `playCount`, the year, the genre, and `starred` (already read). Hearts come from `getStarred2`, which already lists starred songs. Most played comes from `getAlbumList2` with `type=frequent` and then those albums' tracks (`getAlbum`) sorted by play count, since no request lists songs by play count. Genre reads `getAlbumList2` with `type=byGenre` for the chosen genre, and Discover reads it with `type=random`, then each album's tracks (`getAlbum`); Discover sorts them by `playCount` (missing counts as 0), since a shared server may have every track played. On this day scans `getAlbumList2` pages (`LIB-2`'s size of 100) for the OpenSubsonic `releaseDate` (year, month, day) and stops after 20 pages, so a very large library is only partly scanned. Many albums have only a year, so this mix is often empty; that is expected, and it has no card then (`MIX-2`).
- Making the mixes asks the model five times at most, one mix at a time, so the tab shows each card when it is ready.
- A mix keeps no server identity: its screen is pushed with the key `mix:<kind>`, and its tracks come from the phone's copy, not from a request.
